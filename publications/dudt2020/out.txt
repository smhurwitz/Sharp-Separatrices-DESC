          D. W. Dudt
                         1,   a) and E. Kolemen1 , b)
          Princeton University, Princeton, New Jersey 08544
          (Dated: 24 September 2020)
          The new code DESC is presented to solve for fixed-boundary ideal MHD equilibria in stellarators. The approach
          directly solves the equilibrium force balance as a system of nonlinear equations in the form f(x) = 0. The independent
          variables x represent nested magnetic flux surfaces expressed in the inverse representation with toroidal flux coordinates,
          and the equations f(x) quantify equilibrium force balance errors at discrete points in real space. Discretizing with global
          Fourier-Zernike basis functions properly treats the magnetic axis and minimizes the number of coefficients needed to
          describe the flux surfaces. The pseudospectral method provides great flexibility in where the errors are evaluated, and
          the system of equations is efficiently solved with a Newton-Raphson iteration. Equilibria are computed and compared
          against VMEC for both axisymmetric and non-axisymmetric examples. The results show fast convergence rates and
          solutions with low errors throughout the plasma volume.
